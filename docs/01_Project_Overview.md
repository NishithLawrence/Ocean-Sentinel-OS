# 1. Executive Summary

Ocean Sentinel OS is an AI-powered Marine Conservation Decision Intelligence Platform designed for government marine conservation agencies.

The platform integrates marine environmental datasets—including coral reef health, coral bleaching alerts, ghost-net reports, marine protected areas, and weather conditions—into a single enterprise application.

Instead of requiring officers to manually gather information from multiple systems, Ocean Sentinel OS analyzes available data, prioritizes reefs based on environmental risk, recommends conservation actions, and assists officers in planning cleanup missions.

The platform supports decision-making while ensuring that final operational authority remains with human officers.

# 4. Background

Marine ecosystems are under increasing pressure due to climate change, coral bleaching, pollution, ghost nets, and other human activities.

Government agencies often rely on multiple disconnected information sources.

As a result:

- Decision making becomes slow.
- Resources are not always allocated efficiently.
- High-risk reefs may receive delayed attention.

Ocean Sentinel OS addresses these challenges by providing a unified operational platform.

---

# 5. Problem Statement

Current conservation workflows require officers to manually review:

- Coral reef health reports
- Bleaching alerts
- Marine protected area maps
- Weather forecasts
- Ghost-net observations

Because these systems are fragmented:

- Data is difficult to interpret quickly.
- Risk prioritization is inconsistent.
- Mission planning becomes inefficient.
- Reporting requires significant manual effort.

---

# 6. Existing Solutions

Current tools typically specialize in one area:

- Reef monitoring
- Satellite visualization
- Weather forecasting
- GIS mapping
- Research databases

While valuable, these systems generally do not combine operational planning with AI-assisted decision support.

Ocean Sentinel OS aims to bridge that gap by integrating multiple datasets into a single workflow.

# 16. Risks

Technical Risks

- Dataset quality
- Limited hackathon time
- API failures
- Deployment issues

Operational Risks

- Incomplete datasets
- Internet connectivity during demo

Mitigation

- Use sample datasets where necessary.
- Design offline-friendly demo data.
- Keep optional AI features non-critical.

# 19. Glossary

AI
: Artificial Intelligence used for decision support.

Ghost Net
: Abandoned or lost fishing net posing a threat to marine ecosystems.

Marine Protected Area (MPA)
: Legally protected marine region.

Mission
: A conservation operation planned by an authorized officer.

Priority Score
: A calculated score representing the urgency of conservation action.

Reef Intelligence
: Consolidated environmental information and AI assessment for a specific reef.
