# 1. Vision

Ocean Sentinel OS aims to become a comprehensive Marine Conservation Decision Intelligence Platform that enables governments, conservation agencies, and researchers to protect marine ecosystems through data-driven decision making.

The long-term vision is to transform fragmented environmental information into actionable conservation intelligence.

---

# 2. Roadmap Strategy

The roadmap is divided into five phases:

- Phase 1 – Hackathon MVP
- Phase 2 – Production Ready Platform
- Phase 3 – AI Expansion
- Phase 4 – National Deployment
- Phase 5 – Global Marine Intelligence Platform

---

# 3. Phase 1 – Hackathon MVP

Goal

Deliver a functional end-to-end prototype.

Features

- Government Login
- Dashboard
- Interactive Marine Map
- Reef Intelligence
- AI Risk Assessment
- Mission Planner
- AI Team Recommendation
- Analytics
- PDF Reports
- Admin Panel

Status

Completed during the hackathon.

---

# 4. Phase 2 – Production Ready

Objectives

Improve scalability and operational readiness.

Enhancements

- PostgreSQL database
- Cloud storage
- Role-based access improvements
- Audit logging
- Background jobs
- Scheduled dataset updates
- API monitoring
- Better reporting

# 5. Phase 3 – AI Expansion

# 9. Long-Term Vision

Ocean Sentinel OS can evolve from a hackathon prototype into an operational decision-support platform used by environmental agencies to:

- Monitor marine ecosystems
- Prioritize conservation efforts
- Coordinate field operations
- Improve transparency
- Support evidence-based policy decisions

The platform is designed with modular architecture, making future expansion possible without redesigning the core system.
